# Strom Desktop 0.4.0

A quieter home for your heating, with native Qt controls and a little radiator artistry.

## Desktop

- A clearer dashboard with system-palette linework and typography that follows the desktop font.
- A last-completed-run card: heater on-time, estimated cost, and a labeled on-time bar. Reports stay visible during the next run; no invented live telemetry.
- Turn automatic repeats off while a run is active, without interrupting that run.
- Native Heating and View menus, keyboard shortcuts, and compact disclosure controls for advanced options and the activity log.
- English and Spanish support for the new interface.

## Reliability

- Verified relay shutdowns, elapsed-time accounting, delayed heating plans, and thermal constraints covering the full optimization horizon.
- Shorter planning horizons when tomorrow's electricity prices are not yet published, while still rejecting internal coverage gaps.
- Private, atomic credential saves and consistent literal password parsing.
- Update checks no longer leave heating blocked; concurrent windows cannot remove a live update download. Redirect handling and prerelease filtering are corrected.

Strom remains an alpha. Temperature and cost are model estimates. Keep the app running during a cycle; an in-process watchdog cannot protect against a computer crash or power loss.
